Privacy

Effective 5 September 2026

Bramwell is a year planner that runs entirely in your browser on top of your own Google Calendar. This page says exactly what it touches and what it does not.

What Bramwell accesses

When you connect, Bramwell asks Google for one permission: see, edit, share and permanently delete events on your primary Google Calendar (the calendar.events scope). It reads the events of the months you look at, and it creates, edits and deletes events only when you do so in the app — painting a run of days, saving a form, erasing a run.

Bramwell does not request your name, email address, contacts, other calendars, or anything beyond events on your primary calendar.

Where your data lives

  • Your events stay in Google Calendar. Bramwell keeps no copy of its own on any server. There is no Bramwell server: the app is static files served from a content delivery network, and it talks to Google directly from your browser.
  • A cache on your device. To open instantly and to work offline, your browser keeps a copy of recently viewed months and your settings (categories, colours, budgets, mood) in its own local storage. That copy never leaves your device.
  • Your sign-in token lives in memory only and is gone when the tab closes.

What Bramwell never does

  • It does not send your calendar data to anyone, including us.
  • It does not use analytics, advertising, tracking pixels or third-party scripts other than Google's own sign-in library.
  • It does not sell, share or transfer Google user data to any third party.
  • It does not use Google user data to train or improve any model, generalised or otherwise.

Limited Use

Bramwell's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to provide and improve the calendar features you see in the app, and is never transferred to others except as needed to provide those features, for security, or to comply with the law.

Removing access

  • Sign out from the settings sheet in the app. This revokes the token and returns you to the landing page.
  • Revoke Bramwell at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Clear the local cache by clearing this site's data in your browser. Nothing else needs deleting, because nothing else was stored.

Demo mode

The demo uses a generated calendar held in memory. It never contacts Google and writes nothing to your device.
