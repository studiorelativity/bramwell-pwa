About Bramwell

A year planner over your Google Calendar

The reference is the wall-sized year calendar: every day visible at once, marked by hand, so what is planned and what is still open can be read in one glance. Bramwell puts that on a screen and wires it to the calendar you already keep.

What it does

  • A year on one screen. Twelve months as one grid, with a month view that scrolls without page boundaries when you need the detail.
  • Paint, don't type. Pick a category and drag across the days. One run is one all-day event, written straight to your Google Calendar.
  • Budgets that count. Give a category a number of days for the year. The strip keeps the score and tells you the moment you go past it.
  • Blackouts hold. Mark days nothing may be planned over. A run that touches one is refused before it is written.

How it is built

Bramwell is a small, open-source web app with no backend. It is static files on a content delivery network, talking to Google Calendar from your browser with one permission. Your events live in Google; a cache on your device makes it open instantly and work offline. See the privacy page for the full account.

The source is on GitHub under the MIT licence, so you can read it, run it, or deploy your own.
